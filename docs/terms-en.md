## 2. Description of the service

SkillSwap is a platform that helps university students prove their skills:

- You state your professional goal and upload your certificates.
- Artificial intelligence (AI) detects which skills you are missing and generates a certification path.
- You prove each skill with quizzes or mini-projects generated by AI.
- If an attempt is not approved or you disagree with the result, you can escalate the case to a human Verifier.

The service includes a free plan and a paid monthly plan. The limits, prices and conditions of each plan are shown in the app before you subscribe.

## 4. SkillCredits

- SkillCredits are an internal recognition earned by people who verify cases as peer reviewers.
- **They have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money and cannot be transferred.**
- They can only be used for the internal benefits that SkillSwap indicates in the app, and those benefits may change.
- No plan, payment or SkillCredit buys the approval of a skill: the assessments and criteria are the same for everyone.

## 5. Human verification of certificates and mini-projects

- AI performs a first review of your certificates and attempts; it is not the only decision-maker.
- Escalated cases are reviewed by a human Verifier using a rubric by criterion, who records the reasons for the decision.
- Verifiers commit to assess honestly, impartially and confidentially, and to disclose any conflict of interest.
- It is forbidden to submit fake or altered certificates, or to try to deceive the AI or a Verifier. If this happens, we may void the result and suspend the account.
- A verification on SkillSwap is an internal recognition of the platform. It does not replace a degree, a diploma or an official certification issued by an institution.

## 6. Personal data and certificate OCR

We process your personal data in accordance with Peru's **Law No. 29733, Personal Data Protection Law**.

- **What data we process:** name, institutional email, university, professional goal, the certificates you upload, the results of your attempts and basic technical usage data.
- **Certificate OCR:** to read your certificates we use optical character recognition (OCR), which extracts text such as the holder's name, the institution, the date and the certified skill. That text is used to build your path and so the Verifier can review your case.
- **What we use it for:** creating and maintaining your account, generating your path, verifying your skills, improving the service and contacting you about your account.
- **Who we share it with:** we do not sell your data. We may use technology providers (for example, for AI, storage or app distribution) that process it on our behalf, and Verifiers only see what is needed to review your case.
- **Your rights:** you may request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition regarding your data by writing to contacto@skillswap.pe.
- **Retention:** we keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to meet legal obligations.
